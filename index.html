Occupational therapy that comes to you

Steadfast Therapy is a mobile community occupational therapy service that brings professional and compassionate care directly to you – whether at home, in supported accommodation or within your local community.

Meet the OT

Natalie is an AHPRA-registered Occupational Therapist with over 8 years of experience across a wide range of health settings, including in the home, schools, hospitals and the community. At the core of her approach is a commitment to helping her clients enhance their daily lives and achieve their broader life goals. She believes in a holistic, strengths-based approach, working alongside you to build confidence, independence, and practical skills that make everyday life easier and more meaningful.
