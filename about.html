About Natalie

Meet Natalie

Natalie Goh is an experienced Occupational Therapist who loves helping people feel more confident, independent, and capable in their everyday lives. With over 8 years of experience across disability, rehabilitation, aged care, schools, and community settings, she has supported people of all ages to overcome challenges and work towards goals that matter to them.

Known for her warm and approachable nature, Natalie takes the time to get to know each person and what is important to them. She believes the best outcomes come from working together and tailoring support to each individual's needs, strengths, and lifestyle.

Natalie has a special interest in rehabilitation, assistive technology, and home modifications. She enjoys helping people find practical solutions that support independence and make everyday life easier, safer, and more enjoyable.

Outside of work, Natalie enjoys spending time in nature, surfing, travelling, and sharing new food experiences with family and friends.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor of Applied Science (Occupational Therapy) Honours Class I - The University of Sydney
  • AHPRA Registered
  • Member of the Occupational Therapy Australia (OTA)
  • Published Researcher and Oral Presenter: "Thriving in an expanding service landscape: Experiences of occupational therapists working in generic mental health roles within non-government organisations in Australia"
