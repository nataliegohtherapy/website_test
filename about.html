Meet Natalie

Natalie Goh is a warm and compassionate Occupational Therapist with over 8 years of clinical experience across disability, rehabilitation, aged care, schools, and community settings.

She is passionate about building genuine connections and takes the time to understand each person's unique goals, strengths, and challenges. Natalie enjoys working with individuals and families from diverse cultural backgrounds and is committed to providing respectful, inclusive, and person-centred care.

Natalie works with people across all stages of life, from children through to older adults, using a collaborative and strengths-based approach to support meaningful participation in everyday activities. Her professional interests include ADHD and neurodevelopment, sensory regulation, rehabilitation, assistive technology, functional capacity assessments, and promoting confidence, independence, and quality of life.

Outside of work, Natalie enjoys spending time in nature, hiking, travelling, and discovering new food experiences with family and friends.
