What areas do you service?

Natalie currently provides services throughout Northern Sydney, including Chatswood, Pymble, Hornsby, Ryde, and surrounding suburbs. If you're unsure whether your location falls within the service area, please get in touch to discuss available options.

Do I need a referral or diagnosis?

No, you do not need a formal referral or diagnosis to access our services 

What funding options and rebates are available?

Quokka Therapy can provide services to:

  • NDIS self-managed participants
  • NDIS plan-managed participants
  • Support at Home Program (replaced Home Care Package (HCP) program) clients
  • Commonwealth Home Support Programme (CHSP) clients

What ages does Natalie work with?

Natalie works with school-aged children through to adults and older adults.

How long are appointments?

Appointment lengths vary depending on the service being provided. Natalie will discuss suitable appointment options and recommendations during the initial consultation.

Do you have a clinic?

Natalie currently provides mobile services and does not operate from a fixed clinic location. This allows therapy and assessments to take place in the client's home, school, workplace, residential care setting, or community environment. Working within familiar environments often helps achieve more meaningful and practical outcomes.

How can an appointment be booked?

Please email nataliegoh.therapy@gmail.com, and Natalie will get in touch with you about the next steps. Looking forward to working with you!
